# Review of `paper/main.tex` (emgforge technical report, v1)

Sources checked: `main.tex`, the nine figures, `paper/PLAN.md`, `docs/validation/{PLAN,REPORT}.md`, `synthesis/GOLDEN_METHOD.md`, `engines/spatial.py`, `preprocessing.py`, `key_numbers_f{1,2}.json`, and the MUniverse, neural-operator and BMISS papers.

## 1. Blocking issues

1. **Released amplitudes carry a known ×v error.** `spatial.py:239` is `sfap = (csd @ phi) * dz * polarity / cfg.v`; the paper agrees ("dividing by v once too often") yet ships D1–D3 and every µV in Figs 7–8 with the bug in. A one-line fix plus a regeneration (the grid tensor is ~22 min) removes a liability that otherwise taints all amplitude statements, including "several times smaller than recorded ones": after ×4 the pool median is ~24 µV and the max ~160 µV, i.e. roughly physiological, so the abstract's claim may be wrong.
2. **The amplitude chain is undefined.** Table 2 gives "σ_in, a = 1, 0.05 mm" with no unit for σ_in; a = 0.05 mm is a 100 µm fibre (typical 40–80 µm), which alone changes amplitude ×2–4. Nothing says what current the reciprocal φ is normalised to (Fig 6d: "φ (mV)" of what?). `key_numbers_f1.fig3` records `p2p_fem_over_ana_den = 16.8`: the FEM-φ SFAP is 17× the analytical-φ SFAP for the same fibre, hidden by normalisation in Fig 3e. Until units and normalisation are stated, no absolute-scale claim (item 5 of §9, abstract) is checkable.
3. **Scoreboard counts do not match the report's rows.** REPORT tier A lists 20 checks: 15 ✓, 1 ✗, 4 known; the summary (and Table 3) says "14 / 15, known 4". Tier C lists 6 ✓, 1 ✗, 1 known; Table 3 says "4 / 5". Tier S ("7 / 7, known 1") is not in the report at all ("see script output"), although §7 says "the full report with every measured number is in the repository". Define "pass / gated / known" and make the numbers add up.
4. **Numbers that disagree with the report/JSONs:**
   - Abstract: "end-of-fibre potential starts at L/v within 0.4 ms" — Fig 5 caption: "15.4–15.6 ms" for L/v = 15 (0.63 ms late); B4a: 10.51 for 10.
   - §7.1: "invariant to the sampling rate and step (r>0.999, amplitude within 0.2%)" — A0.4a: dz r = 0.99886, p2p 0.9977.
   - §7.1: "Conduction velocity ... 3.94 m/s (pipeline)" — A2.4: 3.98.
   - §5.2 "96.6%" vs §7.4 "96.7%" (JSON 0.9658); "99.96%" vs "99.9%".
   - §6: MUAP scaled "1 + 0.7(θ−0.5)" and warped "1 + 0.18(θ−0.5)" — the trial in `key_numbers_f2.dynamic` used `amp_gain 0.8, warp_gain 0.22`.
   - §4.3 "lagged by 1–2.5 ms" — A0.3: 0.85–2.55 ms.
   - "shift-copies to 1.4×10⁻³" is the *no-denoise* value; golden is 2.2×10⁻³.
5. **Unsupported claims.** "|r| = 1.000 over 105 electrodes × 100 fibres" against NeuroDec (§3.3, §7.4) has no entry in any report or JSON; the absolute value also hides a sign and a scale. "the mesh-resolution study of the earlier pipeline gave r = 0.9998 between 10⁵ and 2×10⁶ cells" — the forearm mesh used here has 42,315 cells, *below* that range, and the paper's central limitation is mesh ripple reaching φ''. "near-identical MUAPs (≈0.26 µV for a matched unit)" — no source, no unit size. "The taper costs r = 0.998" — not in the report.
6. **Abstract promises "surface and intramuscular electromyograms"**; no intramuscular electrode, lead field or signal appears anywhere.
7. **Eq. 2–3 notation.** Eq. 3 defines V_m(z,t) = V_m(vt−|z−z₀|) w(z) (window included), but Eq. 2 then integrates "V_m(z,t) w(z) φ''(z)" — the window appears twice, and the same symbol V_m is used for the one-argument Rosenfalck function and the windowed field. Also Eq. 1 defines i_m without the window while the text says the end terms come from differentiating the *windowed* field.
8. **Fig 6b contradicts the text.** §5.2: harmonic streamlines have "a single junction placed at the muscle's shared innervation zone", then "places the junction at mid-length by default". The figure shows Poisson junctions at z ≈ 126 mm and harmonic at z ≈ 165 mm; the JSON confirms `iz_fraction_mean 0.4997` vs 0.305. The beds compared are not comparable, and the harmonic bed also has a quarter of its fibres spanning <53% of the muscle (`span_fraction q25 0.53`) — unmentioned. "never cross by construction" sits beside "99.96% contained" and a "quality guard".
9. **HD-grid conduction velocity is 8–24% above the set value** (set 4.0; median 4.33, range 4.08–4.95) and is reported as a pass (§7.3, Fig 8a) without stating the set value. On the cylinder the same estimator recovers 3.98. Either fibre obliquity to the grid or the tensor timing is off; say which.
10. **"Fourier engine" and "Farina generator" are conflated in §4.3.** The EOF lead of 2.5 ms (A2.3) and the positive main lobe (B1) are findings on the *Farina generator*, not on the Fourier engine fed the same φ. §7 gets this right; §4.3 does not.
11. **Fibres are shared between units** (`mean_mus_per_fibre 17.6`, 11,207 fibre-slots on a 637-fibre bed). This is a defensible coarse sampling but is never stated; it makes "p2p ∝ n^1.04" nearly tautological and affects cancellation.
12. `\author` block: "Contributions of P.M. and D.F. to be stated by the authors." Check the BioMime year (key `ma2025conditional`; the ICLR reference list gives TNNLS 2024).

## 2. Structure and clarity

- **The validation section reads as a list.** §7 is bullets of r-values. Put every check in one table (id / measured / criterion / result — the REPORT already has it) and use the prose for the three findings that matter: the engine equals the integral; the Fourier/Farina family disagrees; FEM amplitude and the depth law are open. State up front that tier A0 tests the *discretisation of the same integral with the same IAP* — it is a code check, not a physics validation; the abstract currently reads as the latter.
- **Golden method:** the step list is good, but step 1 leans on §7.2 ("as we show in Sec. tierB, halves the spectral content") before the reader has met it; step 5's "The taper costs r = 0.998" needs its source. Fig 3e undercuts the argument: denoising moves r only 0.909 → 0.929 while the remaining FEM-vs-analytical shape difference is larger than the ripple. Say so.
- **§3.2's σ_s discussion and §4.3 are validation material**; move them into §7. §3.3 and §7.4 repeat the NeuroDec sentence verbatim; the 1/v constant is explained four times.
- **Missing definitions:** IED (acronym never introduced), p2p, RTE, SD/DD (defined as words in §6, used as acronyms in §7), "Radon section", "tripole", "C-04 flag" (internal jargon — cut), "known" flag semantics, the RTE formula ("range 50" but first unit at 0.0). Say how the analytical φ is "inverted" from the transfer function, and what the reference electrode is for a zero-mean source (a uniform volume sink ≡ average reference; this is why Fig 2c needs a "baseline uncertainty" band).
- **Tone:** "reported honestly", "deliberately plain", "kept honest" recur; state findings and let them speak. "Golden" is branding — define it once ("the reference recipe") or drop it.
- Split the 26-line abstract into "what it is" and "what was found". The Table 1 skin conductivity jumps from 1.0 (validation) to 4.55×10⁻⁴ S/m (production), a factor 2000, without comment.

## 3. Positioning

- **NeuroDec / Maksymenko 2023:** the honest framing is "same physics on the same anatomy at the lead-field level; the synthesis integral is now checked against first principles; no experimental confrontation yet, whereas NeuroDec was checked against RMS and decomposition." Write it that way in the intro, not only in §9.
- **BioMime / NeuroMotion:** "the twin is usually closed" and "inherit whatever the twin got right or wrong" read as a swipe at a co-author's group's work that was itself validated (1.8% nRMSE; movement EMG). Position as complementary: emgforge is the open twin from which a surrogate can be trained — which is exactly what the ICLR paper does; cite its Fig 2 (r falls to 0.64 for deep fibres) as the concrete instance of the φ'' sensitivity.
- **MUniverse:** its synthetic sets come from NeuroMotion only; emgforge as a second, inspectable generator diversifies the benchmark — say that explicitly.
- **BMISS:** the conclusion's point (check the generator before inverting it) is right; one sentence suffices.
- **Farina 2004:** the report claims the field's reference model has an inverted main lobe and an early EOF. Say "our line-by-line port" every time, note the original was verified against limiting cases and by Maksymenko (NMSE 3–5%), and keep "until audited". Reporting the depth exponent 2.04 (Farina) vs 2.94 (pipeline) only in a figure legend, unexplained, is the kind of thing a reviewer pounces on — also because Roeleveld 1997 found all models decay *faster* than measurements.
- **Missing related work:** Petersen & Rostalski 2019 (open, planar, monopole-free sources — the nearest "open validated model"); Klotz/Röhrle multi-domain models; Mesin's non-stationary/fibre-shortening models (for §6's modulation layer); Carriou 2016; Lowery 2002 on finite-length and Neumann effects.
- **Over-claiming:** "all agree with the quantitative EMG literature" — most tier-B criteria are qualitative (monotone, ordering, "is a power law"); "the twin is usually closed"; "reproduces first principles exactly".
